Plain English Explanation

AQI Lag values (1h & 3h) have the absolute highest impact on forecasts. This means the model heavily relies on the very recent state of the atmosphere (temporal auto-correlation) to predict the next hour.

PM2.5 24-hour rolling averages help the model capture the daily baseline trend of particulate matter levels, smoothing out any sharp hourly fluctuations.

Diurnal cycle (hour) & temperature act as fine-tuning parameters, explaining minor changes in AQI due to daylight progression, human activities (traffic peaks), and thermal dispersion.
